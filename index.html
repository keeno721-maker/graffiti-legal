GraFeeti turns 8,500+ real mapped streets into pins. Run to one, claim it, and your graffiti tag goes up on its wall for everybody to see.

Download on the App Store

Free. iPhone and Apple Watch. Massachusetts only.

How it works

01 · RUN

Pick a pin and go

Start a run on your phone or straight from your Apple Watch. Pass within 75 metres of a pin and you have hit it.

02 · CLAIM

Spend your miles

Split the run across the pins you hit. More miles on a pin means a better shot at the top of it.

03 · HOLD IT

Keep coming back

Street cred is your miles adjusted for pace and how often you return. Stop showing up and somebody takes the wall.
