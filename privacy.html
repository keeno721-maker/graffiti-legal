GraFeeti Privacy Policy

GraFeeti is a GPS running game played across Massachusetts.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy describes what GraFeeti collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. It covers the GraFeeti iOS app and its Apple Watch companion.

The short version. GraFeeti records your runs so it can score them. That means location data, including while the app is in the background during a run. Your data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with data brokers or analytics companies. You can delete your account, and everything that identifies you, from inside the app at any time.

GraFeeti does not collect contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or your device's address book. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, GraFeeti writes your completed runs to Apple Health: the workout, distance, active energy burned, and the route.

On your iPhone, GraFeeti reads nothing from Apple Health. It does not request read permission at all.

On your Apple Watch, GraFeeti reads three things, and only while a run is actually in progress: heart rate, walking and running distance, and active energy burned. These are the values the watch shows you during a run and records into the finished workout — Apple's workout system cannot save a distance or a calorie figure it has not been allowed to read. Nothing is read before a run starts or after it ends.

GraFeeti cannot see any other health information. Not your weight, sleep, steps, blood pressure, medical records, or anything else. It does not request permission to, and the three values it does read are listed above.

Health data is never used for advertising, never used for marketing, and is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party, including data brokers. It is not used for any purpose other than showing your run as it happens and recording it in Apple Health on your own device.

Health permission is optional and granted per item, so you can allow some and refuse others. Declining does not affect anything else in the app, though a run recorded on the watch may then save to Health without a distance or calorie figure. You can change any of it at any time in the Health app under your profile picture → Privacy → Apps → GraFeeti.

What your location is used for, and what it is not

Location is used to score runs and nothing else. It is not used to build an advertising profile, is not sold, and is not shared with data brokers.

Your run start and end points

Because a run often starts at home, GraFeeti trims the beginning and end of your route before showing it to anyone else. This is on by default. You can change it in the app. Other users never see your untrimmed start or end point.

What other players can see

  • Your username, tag, rank, street cred, XP, and total miles.
  • Your runs in the activity feed — distance, duration, date and pins reached.
  • Your route, with the start and end trimmed unless you turn that off.

Your email address is never shown to other players. Neither is your heart rate or any other health figure — those stay on your own device and in your own Apple Health.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open your profile, scroll to Account, and choose Delete account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Deletion permanently removes:

  • your login and email address;
  • your username, profile and graffiti tag;
  • every GPS route, start point and end point you have recorded;
  • your follows.

Finished months keep an anonymised record of the mileage itself, with no name, email, account or location attached to it, so that other players' past results do not silently change. This residual data cannot be linked back to you or to any device, and cannot be used to identify you.

Runs you have already written to Apple Health belong to you and stay on your device. Delete them in the Apple Health app if you want them gone.

Data retention

Your account and runs are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, identifying data is removed at that moment rather than on a schedule.

Children

GraFeeti is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes by Supabase Authentication and are never visible to GraFeeti. No system is perfectly secure, and no absolute guarantee is offered.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Deletion is available directly in the app. For anything else, contact us using the address below.
